# Acceptance Checklist

Use this checklist before a motion proof, animatic, or final delivery. Do not run checks unrelated to the current decision.

## Blocking Technical Checks

- [ ] The deliverable decodes completely.
- [ ] Resolution is `1440x1080`, frame rate is `30fps`, and display aspect is native `4:3`, unless an approved exception is logged.
- [ ] Video and audio streams are present for a final deliverable.
- [ ] There are no unintended black frames, missing assets, long digital silences, clipped peaks, or broken glyphs.
- [ ] The opening book cover is visible in full and holds through the title pause.
- [ ] Full subtitles cover the narration and are mathematically centered at `x=720` for a 1440px-wide frame.
- [ ] Dark text never disappears into dark photographic or graphic material.
- [ ] External materials have local provenance and rights records.

## Direction Checks

- [ ] The opening identifies the book and central claim quickly.
- [ ] A counterintuitive proposition or consequential question creates forward pressure.
- [ ] Every scene has one primary claim, one semantic verb, and one visible result.
- [ ] Every real asset is representative of the claim rather than generic coverage.
- [ ] Downloaded material has been composed, processed, or separated for its planned action.
- [ ] Evidence remains the subject; graphics do not replace reality with a generic infographic.
- [ ] Prominent lines, labels, camera moves, and sound cues have a clear function.
- [ ] Motion contains setup, action, reaction, and result rather than only entrance animation.
- [ ] Quick actions are quick, mechanisms are readable, and conclusions have intentional holds.
- [ ] Camera moves only for a change in attention, scale, or causal relationship.
- [ ] The full sequence does not read as presentation slides or a string of posters.
- [ ] The episode has not copied signature devices or layouts from a previous book without a narrative reason.

## Voice and Mix Checks

- [ ] Narration plays at natural `1.0x` unless an approved change is logged.
- [ ] Thought units sound continuous and do not repeatedly restart at sentence boundaries.
- [ ] Contrast, progression, and conclusion words receive audible but restrained emphasis.
- [ ] The spoken book title is followed by about one second of intentional pause.
- [ ] Scene-change boundaries do not cut active syllables into digital silence.
- [ ] Music is clearly audible in suitable gaps and yields smoothly under narration.
- [ ] The mix is judged by listening as well as integrated loudness and true peak.

## Revision Integrity

- [ ] The observed problem and responsible layer are stated.
- [ ] Accepted layers are explicitly frozen.
- [ ] The patch changes the smallest necessary surface.
- [ ] Provider, runtime, voice, music, and approval changes are appended to the decision log.
- [ ] Audio-only revisions preserve the approved video stream when possible.
- [ ] The version name and unique change are recorded.

## User Handoff

- [ ] Lead with a directly playable video or audio sample.
- [ ] State what changed and what did not change.
- [ ] Tell the user what subjective issue to inspect.
- [ ] Keep internal reports available for audit without requiring the user to interpret JSON.
